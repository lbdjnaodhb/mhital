# Lymphocytes Percentage Normal Range - Hone Health Biomarkers

What Is Lymphocytes (%)?

Lymphocyte percentage reflects the relative balance of lymphocytes compared to other white blood cells. There are three main types of lymphocytes:

B cells, which produce antibodies

T cells, which destroy infected cells and regulate immune responses

Natural killer (NK) cells, which attack infected or abnormal cells

In a blood test, the lymphocyte percentage differs from the absolute lymphocyte count, which measures the total number of lymphocytes circulating in the blood.

It’s important to compare lymphocyte percentage against absolute lymphocyte count because during a viral infection, lymphocytes typically rise while neutrophils fall. A shift in lymphocyte percentage can help clinicians distinguish viral from bacterial causes even when absolute numbers may look relatively normal.

Why Do Lymphocytes (%) Matter?

A clinician will look at the lymphocyte percentage to:

Differentiate between viral and bacterial infections

Understand how well your immune system is responding to viruses, bacteria, and abnormal cells

Detect the presence of an active infection, chronic stress, or an inflammatory condition
