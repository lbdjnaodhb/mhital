# A Bad Breakup Motivated Me to Start Working on My Style

Najem Al-Hasan is a 48-year-old building engineer living in Edinburgh, Scotland. Never much for thinking about fashion, a bad breakup had him searching for a fresh start. With the help of a stylist, he got his style game on point. Here, in his own words, is how he did it.

I once heard some workers call me “the American Eagle hoodie guy.” They were right—I didn’t have much of a style. I was going around in off-the-rack clothes that just didn’t fit me, even as I admired guys like David Beckham and Ryan Reynolds for their clean, sharp looks.

A messy break-up had me looking for a reboot. I’d put on a bit of weight during the pandemic, and I wanted to get back to looking and feeling my best. I started kickboxing and Brazilian jiu-jitsu, working with a trainer and doing HIIT as well. I went from 169 pounds down to 143. Getting leaner was a big goal, and I decided around the same time to revamp my personal style. Or, in my case, actually establish one.

I started working with Patrick Kenger from Pivot Image Consulting after seeing his name in articles and being really impressed by the testimonials on his website. I signed up for an introductory consultation, which blew me away with the level of detail and explanation. I give him all the credit for my transformation.

[on My Style](https://skinsequence.escortskhi.com/)

I really was starting with the basics—like using a color palette to match my look with my wardrobe. (I’m a dark autumn.) Knowing what colors worked for me really helped, as did Patrick’s advice on skincare and hair styling. Just getting my sizing down was a step in the right direction. And I learned what a huge difference tailoring can make; it’s so important to have mass-produced clothing altered to fit you as an individual.

[Me to](https://defensecascade.china-akan.com/)

My closet got a complete makeover. I discovered Nudie Jeans, Reiss, Spoke, and a whole world of new brands. So much that I hadn’t considered before—polos, denim shirts, slim fit jeans—is now part of my repertoire, and I feel confident choosing a look that works for me. We’ve been experimenting with accessories, like hats and bracelets. It’s a work in progress, and I’m enjoying finding myself through it. Right now I’m really enjoying my All Saints biker leather jacket.

Everyone I know has said I look younger; I think of it as tidying things up. People on the street have commented that I look like I’m modeling on the catwalk, and women have approached me to compliment my style. Granted, they tend to be older women who refer to me as “son,” but a flattering comment is a flattering comment!

I’ve really learned that style is something anyone can pick up. Don’t feel that you have to have stereotypically chiseled good looks to look your best. Every person can have their own style, so find and embrace yours. Keeping physically fit helps give you the confidence and energy to really make the most of your style potential; from there it’s just a matter of working with a stylist, or even just teaching yourself.

[Working on My](https://incretin-path.escortskhi.com/)

Most of all, keep an open mind. Be positive—it might take some time to find what works for you. It’s like any project, though: the more you learn, the more confident you’ll be. Don’t be afraid to ask friends and family for feedback; keep an eye on what’s trending, and think about what you like and why. It’s a different journey for everyone, but with the right mindset you’re sure to find your own way.

—As told to Jesse Hicks
