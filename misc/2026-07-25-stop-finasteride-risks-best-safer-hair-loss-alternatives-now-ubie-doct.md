Is there a safer alternative to finasteride for hair loss?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Topical treatments such as topical dutasteride and topical finasteride may serve as safer alternatives to oral finasteride for treating hair loss. Applied directly to the scalp, these options can reduce DHT locally while potentially minimizing systemic side effects like sexual dysfunction, mood changes, and hormonal disruption commonly associated with oral finasteride.

If you're experiencing hair loss and unsure which treatment path is right for you, it's worth understanding the underlying cause first. Hair thinning can stem from genetics, hormones, stress, nutritional deficiencies, or medical conditions—each requiring a different approach.
