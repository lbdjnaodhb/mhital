# This '90 Day Fiance' Star Is Now a Full-Blown Bodybuilder

[Now Full-Blown](https://copperglow.dns0663.com/blog/3144182079.html)

90 Day Fiancé star Anfisa Nava is known for her apparently messy relationship with her husband Jorge Nava, depicted on the trashterpiece TLC reality show. While Jorge was recently sentenced to two and a half years in prison on charges of possession of drugs with intent to sell, Anfisa has found a new calling that has nothing to do with her romantic drama.

Nava has committed herself to bodybuilding. In a lengthy recent Instagram post, she explained her new hobby and what it has to do with everything else going on in her life.

[This Day Fiance' Star](https://regeneration-desk.hbsnzj.com/blog/0072067145.html)

"Jorge and I are not just husband and wife but we are truly best friends and we used to share everything with each other and since we aren’t together physically now and only get to talk to each other once a day it feels like a big part of me was taken away," she wrote in the caption of her husband's prison sentence.

"I don’t normally talk about it because I don’t like to show my emotions to the public, especially if I believe that it will make me look weak and pathetic. It’s just the way I am—I’d rather be thought of as a 'cold hearted bitch' than a 'crybaby,'" she added.

Explaining her passion for bodybuilding, she wrote, "To fill the void that I felt after Jorge was sentenced and I was left on my own I decided to try to stay busy and do what I enjoy the most and it was working out. And ONE DAY I want to take it on a higher level and compete in NPC bikini division."

Nava does yoga and posted an ab workout on her Instagram along with a photo of her evidently doing bicep curls with dumbbells.

[This Day](https://hostdefense-journal.escortskhi.com/)

She finished her note about her fitness pursuit with a reference to those who "try to knock me down," and saying she's going in a "different direction" and is no longer a part of 90 Day Fiancé. Though leaving 90 Day Fiancé behind is easier said than done, given how rabid the online fandom for the show is and how many of her 301,000 followers discovered her through it.

[This Day Fiance' Star](https://hostdefense-journal.escortskhi.com/)

Nava has posted a number of fitness-related photos recently showing off her physique and a dedication to staying healthy. On 90 Day Fiancé, she proved she could keep people talking, but now it looks like they'll be talking less about her relationship and more about her weightlifting form.
