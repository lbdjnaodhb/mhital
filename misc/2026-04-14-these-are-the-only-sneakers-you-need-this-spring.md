# ​These Are The Only Sneakers You Need This Spring

Spring is high time for sneakerheads. Whether you’re into sporty high-tops, stylish leather low-tops, or anything in between, now is the time to show them off. But how do you set yourself apart from your friends? The key is nailing the details of your shoes.

On a trend-finding mission to PROJECT—a top menswear trade show in Las Vegas—we saw one footwear trend that will rise above the rest this year: tonal sneakers (i.e. those with the similar colored soles and uppers).
