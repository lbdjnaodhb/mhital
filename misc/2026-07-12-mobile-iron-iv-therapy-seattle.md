Iron IV therapy delivers iron directly into the bloodstream through an intravenous infusion. Because it bypasses the digestive system entirely, your body absorbs the iron far more efficiently than it would through oral supplements. There is little to no risk of it being lost before it can be used. Drip Hydration administers Venofer Iron IV, a clinically established form of intravenous iron sucrose formulated for rapid and effective absorption. What are the benefits of Iron IV therapy?

Iron IV therapy from Drip Hydration supports your body across a range of functions that depend on healthy iron levels. You may notice improvements in energy and stamina, enhanced cognitive function, better exercise tolerance, and stronger hair and nails. What can Iron IV therapy help with?

If you’re dealing with symptoms tied to iron deficiency or iron-deficiency anemia, in-home IV therapy can be an effective path to relief. Is Iron IV safe in pregnancy?

When iron deficiency is identified during pregnancy, iron IV is sometimes the most effective way to address it. A clinical trial presented at the Society for Maternal-Fetal Medicine’s 2025 annual meeting found that a single intravenous dose administered early in the second trimester was both safe and effective in treating anemia during pregnancy. That said, iron IV during pregnancy should always be carried out under the supervision of your OB-GYN.
