# These Silk Shirts Are the Coolest Way to Dress up This Summer

Our product picks are editor-tested, expert-approved. We may earn a commission through links on our site. Why Trust Us?

Here us out: you should add some silk to your summer shirt collection. Sure, old school men's silk shirts look a scotch tacky, but if styled correctly, they're one of the best ways to look and feel cool this summer. Today's best men's silk shirts range from vibrant camp collar shirts to sophisticated dark polo shirts, and they all have a super luxe feel. In other words: there are plenty of men's silk shirts to fit into any guy's wardrobe.

The one catch with silk is that it can get pretty pricey. Thankfully budget-conscious menswear brands have been blending silk with other lightweight, synthetic fabrics to bring the price down. Rayon, also called viscose, is an artificial textile that has been used to replace silk since the 1950s. You are probably most familiar with this fabric from Hawaiian shirts, which are traditionally made from rayon. Then there is Tencel, which is a recently developed textile made from wood pulp. Both closely mimic the feel of silk while providing the added bonus of being vegan. So whether you go synthetic or the real deal, the best men's silk shirts are a warm weather staple to dress up all your looks.
