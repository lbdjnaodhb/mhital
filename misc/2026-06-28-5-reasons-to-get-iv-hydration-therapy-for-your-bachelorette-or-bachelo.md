Anti-Hangover Tips for Your Bachelorette or Bachelor Party

When you’re enjoying the moment, it’s easy to have a drink or two too many. With some planning and these hangover prevention tips, you don’t have to compromise your bachelorette or bachelor party to avoid waking up with a hangover in the morning.

Drink water: Staying hydrated is the best way to avoid a hangover. You generally want to aim for one glass of water between alcoholic beverages.

Eat some food: Whether you have to raid your host’s fridge or order a pizza, make sure to eat during the night. Food restores nutrients and vitamins that you lose while you’re drinking and helps your body process alcohol.

Know your limits: Does one alcoholic beverage get you lit? Or does it take more than that? Know your limits and don’t exceed them; your body will thank you in the morning!

Set a drink limit: Especially important if you are driving home, make a decision before the start of the party about how many drinks you will have and don’t go past that number. Enlist a good friend to hold you to this limit if necessary!

Pace yourself: While some bachelorette or bachelor parties are all about throwing caution to the wind, consider pacing yourself. You can still have a great time without incurring a hangover.
