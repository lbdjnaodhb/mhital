Internal Medicine

Spinal pain in multiple myeloma is typically managed with a combination of pain-relieving medications, physical therapy, and targeted treatments that address the underlying cause. Common approaches include analgesics (such as NSAIDs or opioids), bisphosphonates to strengthen bone, radiation therapy to shrink painful lesions, and procedures like vertebroplasty or kyphoplasty to stabilize damaged vertebrae. Treatment is tailored to each patient based on the source and severity of the pain.

Because spinal pain has many possible causes—ranging from muscle strain to serious conditions like multiple myeloma—identifying the reason behind your symptoms is the critical first step toward relief.
