Prescription for Chronic Pain

Because of how debilitating pain can be, most people with chronic pain take prescription medications. However, it’s easy to become overly reliant on pain medications, which could lead to addiction. It is also possible to overdose on prescription pain medications by accidentally taking too much or more frequently than prescribed.

In addition to being able to write you a medication prescription for chronic pain, concierge doctors can help you monitor your medication usage. This will ensure that you are taking your medications correctly and in the proper dosages so that you do not overdose or develop an addiction.

Physical Therapy Referrals for Pain

While prescription medications are valuable for occasional relief, natural methods, like physical therapy, are also essential for managing chronic pain. Depending on the cause of your pain, physical therapy can get to the root cause of your symptoms. However, this will require a tailored plan and personal care. To receive physical therapy services, you’ll need a medical referral, which you can receive from a concierge doctor.

Non-surgical Chronic Pain Treatments

Depending on the cause of your chronic pain, surgery isn’t always an option. Instead, you will need to find non-surgical methods to manage your ailment.
