About us

The “Why” Behind LIVV

LIVV is a naturopathic medical clinic, holistic wellness center, and IV hydration lounge established in 2016 by Dr. Jason Phan NMD and Dr. Allison Gordon NMD – a one-stop-shop for all of your natural health solutions. LIVV was born out of the desire to transform healthcare by providing a model focused on preventing disease and promoting optimal health.

[optimal health](https://collagen-atelier.bbsxyxy.com/)

Our inviting space was designed for educating, empowering, and supporting you in reaching your healthiest self. At LIVV, we offer state-of-the-art therapies for chronic disease, performance optimization, regeneration/anti-aging, hormone replacement, and aesthetics. Our naturopathic doctors work with you to investigate your health, identify your root causes of imbalance, and prescribe individualized treatment plans to get you looking, feeling, and functioning your best.

Understanding your body is an adventure and we are here to help guide and support you. We believe that health and confidence are the foundations to LIVV well! We uphold the highest medical qualifications and standards and take pride in our passionate and whole-hearted dedication to natural medicine. Welcome to your destination for ultimate wellness.
