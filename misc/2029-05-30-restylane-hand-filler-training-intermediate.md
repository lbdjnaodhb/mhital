About this lesson

Master hand filler rejuvenation with step-by-step guidance on assessment and safe hand filler injections using Restylane hand filler.

This lesson focuses on subcutaneous cannula placement for the backs of the hands (dorsal hand) to treat crepey skin, soften visible veins and tendons, and restore volume.

Ideal for aesthetic practitioners offering hand filler services and Restylane hand rejuvenation who want predictable, natural outcomes with Restylane for hands.

Things you’ll learn

How to evaluate candidacy and aging signs (atrophy, crepey/dehydrated skin, visible tendons/veins).

How to choose products from the Restylane® portfolio (e.g., Refyne or Volyme, per local labeling) for delicate dorsal-hand tissue and desired outcomes.

Safe, precise subcutaneous hand filler injection technique with a cannula to achieve even distribution and avoid tendon interference.

Protocols to restore thickness and support hydration in aging hands, including considerations for dry or atopic-prone skin.

Grow your hand filler services with a reproducible protocol and clear decision framework.

Improve patient education by clearly articulating hand filler benefits and expected results.

Enhance outcomes with natural contours using Restylane hand filler techniques that your patients will notice.
