Swollen tonsils in children are most often caused by viral or bacterial infections, though allergies, irritants, or chronic conditions can also play a role. Common symptoms include sore throat, difficulty swallowing, fever, and visible redness or white patches. Treatment depends on the cause and may involve rest, hydration, pain relievers, or antibiotics for bacterial infections like strep throat. In recurring or severe cases, a doctor may recommend a tonsillectomy. Because symptoms can mimic more serious conditions, a proper medical evaluation is essential.
