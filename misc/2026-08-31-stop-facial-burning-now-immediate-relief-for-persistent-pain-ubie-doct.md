Sensitive Skin: According to research, people with sensitive skin may experience burning, stinging, or itching. Using skincare products that are gentle and free of irritants can help reduce these symptoms.

Neurocutaneous Disorders: These disorders can cause sensations like burning and are related to nerve issues in the skin. If this is the case, treatments may involve addressing the underlying nerve problem.

Consulting with a healthcare professional can help determine the cause and appropriate treatment.
