# Pre & Post Treatment Care

Pre & Post Treatment Care

TED Hair Restoration Pre-Treatment Instructions

Avoid alcohol

Avoid excessive sun exposure

Discontinue topical scalp products (minoxidil, oils, serums) 24 hours prior

Day of treatment:

Wash hair thoroughly (clean scalp = better absorption)

Do NOT apply hair products or leave-in conditioners

[Post Treatment Care](https://innate-signal.jcesqc8.com/blog/4382413867.html)

Avoid caffeine if you are sensitive (optional)

Medical considerations: Notify us if:

Active scalp infection, psoriasis flare, or open wounds

[Pre Post Treatment](https://reboundworks.escortskhi.com/blog/5782068042.html)

Recent PRP, microneedling or hair transplant

Pregnancy or breastfeeding

TED Hair Restoration Post-Treatment Instructions

No downtime – you may resume most normal activities

Hair may feel slightly damp or coated from serum

First 24 hours:

Do NOT wash your hair

[Post Treatment Care](https://satietybrief.china-akan.com/)

Avoid: sweating, saunas, steam rooms, hot tubs, hair products

After 24 hours:

Resume normal hair washing and styling

[Pre Post Treatment](https://collagen-atelier.bbsxyxy.com/)

You may restart topical treatments (unless otherwise directed)

What to Expect:

No pain, needles, or downtime

Subtle improvement initially

Best results seen after a series (typically 3 treatments spaced 4 weeks apart)

Continued improvement over 3-6 months

[Treatment Care](https://defensecascade.china-akan.com/)

Aftercare tips for Best Results:

Consider combining with: nutraceuticals (collagen support, vitamin D optimization)

Maintain a healthy scalp environments

When to Call Us:

Unusual scalp irritation lasting >48 hours

Signs of infection (redness, swelling, warmth, discharge)

Medical Disclaimer: This post‑care information is provided for general guidance and does not replace professional medical evaluation or treatment. If you experience symptoms that may indicate a serious or potentially life‑threatening condition, seek immediate medical care.
