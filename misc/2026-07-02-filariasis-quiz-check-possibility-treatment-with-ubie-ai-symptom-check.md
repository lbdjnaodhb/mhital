What is Filariasis?

[Symptom Checker](https://glycometrix.snaketongueofficial.com/blog/0118622433.html)

Filariasis is a tropical, parasitic disease that impacts the lymph nodes and lymph vessels. The parasite is carried by mosquitoes and spreads to humans through mosquito bites.

Typical Symptoms of Filariasis

Joint pain affecting multiple joints throughout body

Sweating at night requiring change in clothing

Fever

Swollen lymph nodes or lumps in the neck, armpits, groin, or behind the ears

Swollen lymph nodes that persist and grew larger over several weeks

Multiple swollen lymph nodes or lumps in neck or armpits

Small lumps / swollen lymph nodes in the armpit

Swollen lymph nodes in two or more areas

Diagnostic Questions for Filariasis

Your doctor may ask these questions to check for this disease:

Do you have joint pain in multiple joints throughout your body?

Do you excessively sweat at night and need to change clothes?

Do you have a fever?

Do you have any swollen lymph nodes or lumps in your neck, armpits, groin, or behind your ears?

Do your swollen lymph node persist and grew larger over several weeks?

Treatment of Filariasis

Medication to eliminate the parasites is prescribed. Other treatments, including surgery, may be advised to address limb and scrotal enlargement.
