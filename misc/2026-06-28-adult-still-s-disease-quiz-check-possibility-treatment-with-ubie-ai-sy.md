What is Adult Still's Disease?

A rare systemic inflammatory disease characterized by high fevers, a pink rash, sore throat and body aches that persist for weeks. The exact cause is unknown but is thought to be triggered by an infection. Some patients will have only one episode of it, while others may have several recurrent episodes.

Typical Symptoms of Adult Still's Disease

Fever above 100.4°F / 38°C lasting 7 days or longer

Fever

Multiple areas of skin redness or red spots

Recurrent fever episodes

Swollen lymph nodes or lumps in the neck, armpits, groin, or behind the ears

Multiple swollen lymph nodes or lumps in neck or armpits

Joint pain, swelling, or discomfort

Warmth in the joints

Diagnostic Questions for Adult Still's Disease

Your doctor may ask these questions to check for this disease:

Have you had a fever above 100.4°F / 38°C for 7 days or longer?

Do you have a fever?

Do you have multiple red areas or spots on your skin?

Do you have recurrent fever episodes?

Do you have any swollen lymph nodes or lumps in your neck, armpits, groin, or behind your ears?

Treatment of Adult Still's Disease

It is typically treated with medicine to reduce inflammation and pain. It may also require steroids or medications that dampen the body's immune response.
