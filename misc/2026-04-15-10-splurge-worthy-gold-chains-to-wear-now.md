Miabella 18-Karat Gold Figaro Chain Necklace

Probably the most popular chain of choice for men, a figaro necklace generally comprises two or three small circular links accompanied by a single elongated-oval link. Here’s the proof that a glistening gold chain need not break bank.

Maria Black Kim Gold-Plated Necklace

Copenhagen-based Maria Black’s sleek designs are as versatile as they come, specifically made for you to style together with the rest of your jewelry collection. The brand's “Kim” necklace with a hexagonal pendant is ideal for just that, so you can layer to your heart’s content.

Tom Wood Gold-Plated Chain Necklace

Tom Wood’s Scandi-style aesthetic manifests itself marvelously in this gold-plated sterling silver square chain necklace. Its minimalist vibe plays nice with layering on top of other necklaces. Its clear-cut design still makes it a statement standalone piece.

Miansai Volt Link Paper Clip Necklace

This IG-favorite may be known best for its more modest menswear offerings, but Miansai goes bolder with this quirky—though still refined—paperclip pendant that’s assembled via elongated chain links.

David Yurman 18-Karat Yellow-Gold Box Chain Necklace

You can’t go wrong with a gold chain necklace from one of America’s most renowned jewelry designers. David Yurman’s take on this staple piece consists of box—or square-shaped—chain links in a sparkling 18-karat yellow gold.
