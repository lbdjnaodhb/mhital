# Ric Flair Made His First Appearance Since His Coma, and His T-shirt Pretty Much Says It All

On August 14, WWE legend Ric Flair was placed into a medically-induced coma after being admitted to a hospital to address an intestinal blockage. Eventually, he had to have part of his colon removed. At the time, the mood around Flair's health was somber and serious, as those familiar with situation revealed that he was in dire straits. However, this past Saturday, while many in America were celebrating a long weekend, Flair took to Twitter to celebrate his improved condition and announce one, very important thing: "I ain't dead yet, motherf***ers."

"To all my fans out there, let it be known worldwide that Nature Boy is back up and running," Flair said in a personal address to his supporters. "I want to tell all my fans out there: Thank you for all the love, support, and I will see you in the very, very, very, very, very, very near future."
