The best tool in the fight against Covid-19 is knowledge – not just about how the virus spreads or the symptoms to watch out for – but when it’s time for you to get tested.

You should get tested if…

You’ve been in contact with someone who has a confirmed case of Covid-19, whether or not you have symptoms

You’ve spent time around people who are not socially distancing or wearing masks

If you have flu-like symptoms

If you’ve recently traveled to an area with high rates of infection and want to take extra precautions to protect someone in a vulnerable group at home
