# Tom Brady and Troy Aikman Drink a Shocking Amount of Water Every Day

NFL Hall of Famer Troy Aikman claims to drink up to two gallons of water a day, partly inspired by six-time Super Bowl winner Tom Brady, according to Insider. The three-time Super Bowl winner with with the 1992-1995 Dallas Cowboys turned Fox sports analyst also claims to work out six hours a week and follows the Pegan diet—but is two gallons of water (equivalent to roughly 300 ounces) a day good for anyone?

"While two gallons may be appropriate for a multi-Super Bowl winning athlete, on average this would be too much," says Eric Ascher, M.D., Family Medicine Physician at Lenox Hill Hospital. For the average person, Dr. Ascher recommends 100 ounces a day, or 0.65 gallon give or take, especially if your activity levels are less than that of Aikman's.

Tom Brady is known to drink upwards of 25 glasses of water a day, which is a around 200 ounces, which isn't outlandish for someone of his size and activity level, but might be too much for Aikman.

"For every hour of exercise or sweat session (i.e. hot yoga or a massage), you need an additional 16 to 24 ounces of water," he says. Similarly, if you drink dehydrating drinks like coffee or alcohol, you'll need an additional 16 ounces per two alcoholic drinks or cups of coffee, according to Dr. Ascher.

Drinking water can feel like one of the healthiest things you can do for your body, but there's always too much of a good thing.

"Too much water will cause an imbalance of the essential vitamins and minerals your body needs in order to function," says Dr. Ascher, at worst, putting your brain in overload, causing it to swell, and practically drown in fluid.
