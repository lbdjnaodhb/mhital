# Facial Assessment: Six-Point Protocol & Planning (No Injections)

Preview

About this lesson

Join Dr. Juan Sopena for a structured six-point assessment you can use in every consultation. Working with a Caucasian female case, he reviews shape/contour, expression cues, proportions and profile, dynamics, independent subunits (nose and lips) for budget-friendly staging, and skin quality. The focus is on diagnosis, prioritization, and treatment planning—no injections are shown.

Things you’ll learn

A repeatable six-point facial assessment: shape/contour, expression, proportions/projection, dynamics, subunits, skin quality

How mild temporal hollowing, midface support, and pre-jowl sulcus interact, and why midface lift/support is prioritized first

Identify expressions, like tired/sad cues, from tear troughs.

Evaluate facial proportions and profile to plan balance and projection

Run a quick dynamics screen and note when a neuromodulator may be appropriate in planning

Prioritize subunits and skin quality in case of budget-aware plans
