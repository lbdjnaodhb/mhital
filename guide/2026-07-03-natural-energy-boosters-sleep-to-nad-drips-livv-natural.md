Sleep hygiene: build a foundation

Sleep is when your body repairs itself and refuels. During deep sleep, your cells produce adenosine triphosphate (ATP), and growth hormone is released to rebuild tissue and support metabolism.

When you miss out on deep sleep, you wake feeling foggy and run down. To improve sleep quality, stick to a consistent bedtime, keep your room dark and cool, and avoid screens before bed.

If you struggle with rest, our sleep guide has practical tips for better nights.

Hydration: fuel your cells

Water powers every system in your body.

Mild dehydration—just a 1–3 % drop in body weight—can impair mood, memory, and energy.

Dehydration makes it harder for your heart to pump blood and deliver oxygen, which leads to fatigue.

Keep a reusable bottle with you and sip throughout the day. If you work out or spend time in the sun, drink extra fluids and choose water‑rich foods like cucumbers and oranges.

NAD⁺ therapy
