What Is PIED?

"Porn-Induced Erectile Dysfunction" is a term used online to describe difficulty getting or maintaining an erection during partnered sex while still being able to get erections during pornography use.

It's important to understand:

PIED is not currently an official medical diagnosis.

However, doctors and sexual health specialists do recognize that pornography habits can influence sexual performance in some men.

So when asking, does porn affect erectile dysfunction? The answer is nuanced: it can contribute in certain situations—but it is rarely the only cause.

How Erections Actually Work

To understand the issue, it helps to know how erections happen.

An erection depends on:

Healthy blood flow

Proper nerve function

Balanced hormones (especially testosterone)

Mental and emotional stimulation

Low levels of anxiety or stress

If any of these are disrupted, erectile dysfunction (ED) can occur.

That means ED is often multifactorial—there may be more than one cause.
