Allergens and Additives

Anti-aging supplements come in many forms, most of which contain a combination of ingredients. Some additives are necessary to bind together the ingredients in pill or liquid form.

Artificial colors are added to some supplements to improve their appearance. While not necessarily harmful, artificial colors aren’t required for the supplement to be effective, either. If you’re unsure how an additive contributes to a given supplement, you can always check online or contact the manufacturer with questions.

If you have an allergy, it’s important to check that the supplement you’re considering doesn’t contain an additive that will trigger an allergic reaction.

Are Oral Anti-Aging Supplements Effective?

Oral supplements are absorbed by your digestive system, which impacts how much your body actually absorbs. In fact, oral supplement absorption rates vary from person to person due to factors such as individual circulatory systems, gastric emptying time, and the ingredients within the supplement.

Because supplements are not fully absorbed, the relative bioavailability (the amount of a drug or supplement that your body can actually use) is very low. As a result, whether you’re using a multivitamin or an anti-aging capsule, oral supplements are less effective than IV infusions because of their low absorption rate.
