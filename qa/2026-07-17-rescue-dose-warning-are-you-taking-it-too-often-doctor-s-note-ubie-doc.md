# Rescue Dose Warning: Are You Taking It Too Often? | Doctor’s Note | Ubie Doctor's Note

Published on: 7/21/2026

Can you take a rescue dose too often or too close together?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

[Taking It](https://thymicshield.dns0663.com/blog/6164962022.html)

Internal Medicine

Rescue medication should only be used as directed by your doctor. Using it too often, or taking doses too close together, can cause serious side effects and may signal that your condition isn't well controlled. Common risks of overuse include a rapid heartbeat, tremors, headaches, and reduced effectiveness over time.

[Rescue Dose Warning](https://tissue-renewal.china-akan.com/)

Key points to remember:

Follow your doctor's prescribed dosing schedule exactly

Track how often you use your rescue medication

Frequent use (more than twice a week) may indicate your treatment plan needs adjustment

Never double up on doses to make up for a missed one

If you're relying on rescue medication more than usual, or noticing new or worsening symptoms, it's important to understand why.
