How long should I wait before taking the next dose of acetaminophen (Tylenol)?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Wait at least 4 to 6 hours between doses of acetaminophen (Tylenol) for adults and children. Do not exceed 4,000 mg in 24 hours for adults, or the label-recommended limit for children based on weight and age. Taking doses too close together or exceeding the daily maximum can cause serious liver damage.

Explanation

Acetaminophen, also known as Tylenol, is a common medication used to relieve pain and reduce fever. According to the studies referenced, acetaminophen is usually taken every 4 to 6 hours as needed. This means that after you take a dose, you should wait at least 4 hours before taking another one.

Important Points:

Time Between Doses: Always wait at least 4 to 6 hours before taking the next dose.
