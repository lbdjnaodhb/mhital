What is pediatric urgent care?

Urgent care is usually an office setting, not an emergency room. It is for illnesses or injuries that are not serious enough to go to an emergency room but that still need medical attention right away. A Pediatric Urgent Care office has pediatric doctors on staff who specialize in treating children with injuries and illnesses.

Pediatric urgent care can be found in select US cities. Some practices are open seven days a week and, in some cases, 24 hours per day.

Who should you call?

Pediatricians and other medical practitioners who handle urgent care cases can be found in two main areas: private practices and hospital-based.

Hospital-based professionals may see patients during any hours of operation for their facility, which means they can accommodate late or same-day appointments.

Private practice doctors typically only schedule business hours for appointments, and it may take several days or weeks to get an appointment.

Concierge doctors, meanwhile, provide one-on-one attention to every patient in the comfort of their own home. This means that even patients with acute or chronic diseases can arrange appointments during late hours. Even better, concierge doctors are accessible via telemedicine after hours and over weekends if additional treatment is required.
