Explanation

When you feel pain directly over your heart, it might be a sign that your heart isn't getting enough blood or is under stress. One common cause is angina. Angina happens when the blood flow to your heart muscle is reduced because the arteries are narrow or blocked. This can cause pain during physical activity or stress.

Another serious cause is a heart attack, which occurs when a blockage suddenly stops blood from flowing to a part of the heart. This is an emergency and needs immediate attention. In some cases, the pain might be caused by pericarditis, which is when the lining around the heart becomes inflamed.
