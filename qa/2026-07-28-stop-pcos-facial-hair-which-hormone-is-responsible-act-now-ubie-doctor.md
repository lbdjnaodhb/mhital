Key Points:

Androgens: These are hormones like testosterone that are usually found in higher amounts in men. Women with PCOS have more of these hormones than usual.

Hirsutism: This is the term used to describe extra hair growth on the face and body of women, and it is often caused by these higher androgen levels.

Impact: While everyone has some androgens, women with PCOS have more, which leads to changes like more hair growth.
