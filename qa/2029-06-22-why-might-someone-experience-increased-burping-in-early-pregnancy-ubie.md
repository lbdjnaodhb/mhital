During early pregnancy, many women experience increased burping. This can occur because of hormonal changes that affect the digestive system.

Why Does This Happen?

Hormonal Changes: Pregnancy hormones, like progesterone, relax muscles in the body. This includes the muscles in the digestive tract, which can slow down digestion and lead to more gas build-up.

Stomach Pressure: As the baby grows, there can be more pressure on the stomach, which might push gas up and cause burping.

These changes are common and part of how the body adjusts to support the growing baby. If burping becomes uncomfortable or is accompanied by other symptoms, it might be helpful to talk to a healthcare provider.
