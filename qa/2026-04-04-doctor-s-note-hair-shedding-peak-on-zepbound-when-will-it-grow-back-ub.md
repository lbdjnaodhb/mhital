Doctor's Note: Hair Shedding Peak on Zepbound – When Will It Grow Back?

Starting Zepbound (tirzepatide) can bring welcome weight-loss results, but for some people, a period of hair shedding follows. This guide explains why hair shedding happens on Zepbound, when you can expect it to peak, and practical Zepbound and hair shedding solutions to help you through it.

Why Does Hair Shedding Happen on Zepbound?

Hair grows in cycles: a growth phase (anagen), a transition phase (catagen), and a resting phase (telogen). If more hairs than normal enter the telogen (resting) phase at once—a condition called telogen effluvium—you'll notice increased shedding. Several factors related to Zepbound can trigger this:

Rapid Weight LossLosing more than 5% of body weight in a few months can stress the body, pushing hairs into the shedding phase.

Nutritional ShiftsChanging eating patterns or unintentionally eating too little protein, iron, zinc, or certain vitamins can affect hair health.

Hormonal ChangesZepbound alters blood sugar and insulin levels. These shifts can, in some people, influence hair-growth signals.

Physical and Emotional StressStarting a new medication, adapting to diet changes, or life stressors can all contribute to temporary hair shedding.

Zepbound and Hair Shedding Solutions

You can support healthy hair growth as your body adjusts. Here are practical steps:

1. Nourish from Within

Protein: Aim for 0.8–1.0 g per kg of body weight daily (lean meat, fish, eggs, legumes).

Iron & Zinc: Include fortified cereals, red meat, spinach, pumpkin seeds.

B Vitamins & Biotin: Whole grains, eggs, nuts, and legumes help support growth.

Vitamin D: Get safe sun exposure or consider a supplement on a doctor's advice.

2. Gentle Hair Care

Use a mild, sulfate-free shampoo and conditioner.

Avoid daily heat styling (blow-dryers, curling irons).

Skip tight hairstyles (ponytails, braids) that tug on the hair shaft.

Pat hair dry instead of rubbing aggressively with a towel.

3. Topical Treatments (Discuss with Your Doctor)

Minoxidil: Over-the-counter foam or solution can encourage regrowth.

Low-Level Laser Therapy: Home devices may help stimulate follicles.

Timing: Expect peak shedding around 3–4 months on Zepbound; regrowth often starts by month 5.
