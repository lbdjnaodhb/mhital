Do pregnancy symptoms start earlier in second or third pregnancies? Yes, symptoms like nausea, fatigue, and a visible bump often appear earlier in second or third pregnancies than in the first. This is because your body has already experienced pregnancy, so hormonal shifts and physical changes may be recognized sooner.

Because early pregnancy symptoms can overlap with other conditions, it's important to know exactly what your body is telling you.
