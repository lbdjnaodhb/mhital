What is Clenbuterol?

Clenbuterol was first created to treat asthma, helping to open up a person’s airways during an attack. It’s also been given to livestock to increase their lean body mass. Farmers do this to increase the meat on their animals so they can make bigger profits.

Although clenbuterol’s proven for building muscle in animals, it’s not quite as effective in humans when trying to bulk up. Clenbuterol has anabolic or muscle-sparing attributes – but it’s predominantly a fat burner and only effective for weight loss.

How Does Clen Burn Fat?

Clenbuterol stimulates thermogenesis in the body, resulting in a higher body temperature. This rise in temperature is a natural side effect of higher adrenaline and blood pressure levels.

What happens then is the body works intensely at cooling your body temperature back down (trying to maintain a homeostasis), causing calories to be burned at rest.

Due to this effect, you’ll burn fat on clen even if you sit on your bum all day. However, if you undergo intense exercise whilst taking clenbuterol you’ll get even better results.

What Is Yohimbine?

Yohimbine is derived from the bark of several trees (including pausinystalia and corynanthe ). Yohimbine is very effective at burning fat, but the jury is still out when it comes to its safety over the long term.

Yohimbine is similar in nature to caffeine or clenbuterol in the sense that it’s a stimulant, however it also has antioxidant properties and dilates your blood vessels, allowing more blood to flow freely. It also stabilizes blood sugar levels and reduces cortisol, helping you to burn fat in stubborn areas. For men this is usually at the bottom of the abdomen and in women on the thighs and hips.
