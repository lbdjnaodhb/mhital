It must be a bit warmer where you are than here in New York City. Luck you. To answer your question: Absolutely. With the right amount of confidence and know-how, we believe you can wear just about any unlikely pairing. There’s nothing wrong with a tux jacket and jeans or wingtips with shorts. Just make it situationally-appropriate. In other words, these things aren’t happening at work.

With short sleeves and a vest, you’re on pretty safe turf. Witness well-dressed dudes like Adam Levine, Usher and Ryan Gosling. They’ve all pulled this look off recently. We like tailored vests and a T-shirt with jeans or chinos. The result: a casual, rocker vibe. If you’re seeking a more professional look, a short-sleeved collared shirt (with or without a tie) works well too. Just pay attention to fit. A baggy shirt and vest looks like you’re an off-duty valet in a rental uniform. Keep everything fitted (but not tight) to make this look work for you.
