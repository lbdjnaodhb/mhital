Tingling in the left arm can signal a circulatory problem, such as a heart-related condition or a blockage in the blood vessels of the arm. Because the left arm shares nerve pathways with the heart, tingling here is sometimes an early warning sign of reduced blood flow, nerve compression, or even a cardiac event. Other possible causes include pinched nerves, poor circulation, or vascular disease.
