Treatment for allergy attacks

The treatment for allergy attacks is dependent on what type of allergies you are experiencing. For food allergies and seasonal allergies caused by changes in weather, a medical professional will most likely prescribe an antihistamine to combat the body’s natural production of histamines. Nasal sprays and allergy shots can also help if oral medication is not easing allergy symptoms.

For more severe allergic reactions that result in anaphylaxis, epinephrine should be administered right away.
